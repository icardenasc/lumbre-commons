# Manuscript guide · Lumbre

Prepare your text in the web editor. This file is a writing guide, not an automatic import format.

https://lumbre-commons.ia-cardenas10.workers.dev/en/submit

## Title
Identify the question and contribution without overstating findings.

## Abstract
State the problem, objective, method, findings or argument, and main limitations.

## Question and context
Explain prior knowledge, the gap and your question. Cite verified sources.

## Method
Describe design, corpus, source selection, procedures and checks. In legal research identify jurisdiction and validity dates; in empirical studies explain samples, measurements, exclusions and uncertainty.

## Results or argument
Separate observations, interpretation and hypotheses. Do not claim experiments that were not conducted. For an open question, explain what would count as a satisfactory answer.

## Limitations
Scope, uncertainty, bias and alternative explanations.

## Main claims
Up to eight, one per line, suitable for a linked response.

## References
Use a consistent style. Include verified authors, titles, dates and DOI or URL.

## AI disclosure
Name tools, models, tasks and human checks, or state that AI was not used.

## Funding, conflicts and ethics
Complete each separate field. Explain funding, relevant interests, approvals, consent and safeguards, or why they do not apply.

## Data and materials
Save the draft and attach up to eight files of 5 MB each, or link to repositories. Explain access restrictions and third-party licences.

## Before submission
Select the original language. Check the preview, save, confirm author and coauthor rights, and agree to CC BY 4.0 for the text. Monitor editorial decisions in your workspace.
